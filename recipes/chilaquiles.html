Chilaquiles

About This Dish

Chilaquiles remind me of a Sunday morning. You wake up and instantly smell the tortillas being fried. I would always try and make it into the kitchen before the chips were drowned in the tomato sauce. Those fresh, warm chips were the best. You knew it was going to be a good day.

Ingredients

  • Casa Sanchez Thick Tortilla Chips
  • El Pato Hot Tomato Sauce
  • Heavy Wipping Cream
  • Queso Fresco
  • Onion
  • Cilantro
  • Egg
  • Avocado*

Steps

  1. Mix entire El Pato sauce with 2 oz of heavy whipping cream and heat in a sauce pan at medium-high for three minutes until hot.
  2. While sauce is heating, dice half an onion and finely chop cilantro.
  3. Fry a single egg to your liking.
  4. Fill a bowl 3/4 full with Casa Sanchez chips.
  5. Add enough sauce to cover all chips in the bowl.
  6. Sprinkle a good amount of diced onion and queso fresco on top of the bowl.
  7. Once egg is fried to your liking, place on top of the chips and sprinkle some cilantro to complete this meal.
    8. * If you'd like, feel free to add sliced avocado to take this to the next level!

* Optional
