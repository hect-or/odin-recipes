Paloma

About This Drink

I present you with the Paloma to continue this introduction of Mexican staples. This drink is made up of a mixture of grapefruit soda and a spirit made exclusively in the Mexican state, Jalisco

Ingredients

  • Centenario Reposado Tequila
  • Squirt Grapfruit Soda
  • Penafiel Soda Water
  • Lime Wedges
    • * Tajin or Salt

Steps

  1. Fill cocktail glass halfway with ice.
  2. Measure 1.5 oz (3 oz for a Double) of Centenario and pour into cocktail glass.
  3. Pour soda water into cocktail glass and fill 3/4 of the way.
  4. Finish with is much Squirt as you'd like.
  5. Garnish with a lime wedge.
    6. * For added flavor add a rim of Tajin or salt to your glass.

* Optional
