Flautas

About This Dish

Crispy chicken taquitos, what's not to love? This is another childhood staple of mine that is really easy to make! Flauta means flute in spanish.

Ingredients

  • Corn Tortillas
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Rottiserie Chicken
  • Toothpicks
  • Sour Cream
  • Queso Fresco
  • Lettuce
    • * Hot Sauce

Steps

  1. Shred rottiserie chicken to use for filling flautas.
  2. Chop lettuce to use for topping.
  3. Heat tortillas for about a minute to make them easier to handle while rolling flautas.
  4. Once tortilla is warm, place a layer of shredded chicken across the middle of the tortilla.
  5. While being careful, roll the tortilla as tight as possible like a joint and use a toothpick to prevent it from unrolling.
  6. Repeat steps 3-5 to assemble as many flautas as you'd like.
  7. While rolling flautas, fill a pan with a couple inches of vegetable oil and bring up to frying temperature.
  8. Use tongs to place flautas in hot oil and flip to the other side after a couple minutes until both sides are golden.
  9. Line a plate with paper towels to soak up excess oil.
  10. Once flauta is golden on the exterior, use tongs to pick up and drain excess oil before placing on plate lined with paper towels
  11. Repeat steps 8-10 to fry as many flautas as you'd like.
  12. Let flautas cool for a few minutes before plating.
  13. REMOVE TOOTHPICKS
  14. Once flautas are plated, top with lettucee, queso fresco and a dollop of sour cream to finish!
    15. * Add your favorite hot sauce for an extra kick.

* Optional
